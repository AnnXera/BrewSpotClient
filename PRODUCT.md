# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Business owners managing multiple locations who need to oversee and coordinate operations across different coffee shops.

## Product Purpose
Streamline operations and manage customer subscriptions for coffee shops.

## Positioning
Brewspot provides a unified platform specifically tailored for multi-location coffee shop owners to manage operations and subscriptions efficiently.

## Operating Context
Used in back-office environments by business owners, likely on desktop web browsers, to configure subscriptions, monitor shop performance, and manage overarching operations.

## Brand Commitments
Must preserve the existing brand identity, including logo and colors.

## Product Principles
- **Efficiency at Scale**: Design for managing multiple locations simultaneously.
- **Clarity and Control**: Provide clear overviews of operations and straightforward management tools.
- **Brand Consistency**: Maintain the established Brewspot brand identity across the application.
